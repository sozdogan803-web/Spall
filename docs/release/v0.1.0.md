# Spall 0.1.0

First public release. Spall is a Windows viewer for armour vs projectile terminal ballistics: one
projectile against a stack of generic plates, with an engineering model and an optional 2D
plane-strain deformation simulation (MLS-MPM on the GPU).

## Download

`Spall-0.1.0-win64.zip`: unzip anywhere and run `Spall.exe`. It needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
and a GPU with OpenGL 4.6 drivers. No installer, no runtime to install. The exe is unsigned, so
Windows SmartScreen warns the first time ("More info", then "Run anyway").

## What is in it

- Projectile catalogue: long rods, compact AP shot, shells, missiles and shaped-charge jets, each
  entry with sources and a confidence.
- Engineering model: Tate-Alekseevskii long-rod penetration, de Marre / Lambert-Jonas for compact
  AP, jet stretching and breakup for shaped charges, obliquity, spaced plates, ceramics.
- Deformation view: rods mushroom and erode, plates bulge and fail, ceramics comminute,
  laminates delaminate, reactive tiles are draggable blocks, plus ballistic gelatin and witness
  sheets. Quality from Low to Ludicrous (up to 100 million particles, best pre-rendered).
- Pre-render the animation, then scrub or play it at any speed in both directions.
- Cases (save and load setups), CSV export of the time histories, F12 screenshots.

## Known limits

- 2D plane strain, generic material constants: a round rod is a slab, a perforated plate shows as a
  row of slots, gelatin has no rate hardening or cavitation.
- The engineering model is 1D along the shot line (no ricochet or nose reflection), so above about
  70 degrees obliquity its verdict is outside what it can say. The two views can disagree (the
  M993 carbide-core rod shatters in the deformation view and perforates in the engineering model).
- Five explosive fillers in the catalogue (TNA, PBXN-107, PBXN-103, Destex, a generic thermobaric
  mix) have no source and are marked as estimates; no projectile uses them.
- The shaped-charge liner collapse is not simulated; a HEAT warhead body is not part of this
  release.
- Tested on one machine (NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti). AMD and Intel GPUs are untested, and the free
  video-memory query is NVIDIA only.

Educational tool, not a design or certification tool. Outputs are physical quantities only
(penetration, residual velocity, plate deformation); there are no damage, lethality or targeting
outputs.
